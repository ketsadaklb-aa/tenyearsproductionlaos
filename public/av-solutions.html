How we deliver

Design. Supply. Install. Maintain.

One accountable team from first site visit to the years of service after handover — no passing you between a designer, a supplier and a contractor.

Design

We survey the space, establish what it has to do, and specify a system that suits the room's acoustics, sightlines and budget — not a copied equipment list.

Supply

Equipment sourced through established manufacturer channels, with the documentation and warranty terms institutional procurement requires.

Install

Cabling, rigging, mounting, power and control installed properly and commissioned — tuned, tested and signed off against the agreed specification.

Maintain

Scheduled servicing, operator training and parts support, so the system still performs as specified years after handover.
